# Overview

Welcome to your personal LLM Wiki (Second Brain), fully integrated into your AIS-OS! This wiki is a persistent, compounding knowledge base maintained by your AI agent.

## Purpose
- **Compounding Synthesis**: Instead of retrieving isolated chunks from raw documents at query time (standard RAG), the agent builds and maintains a network of interlinked markdown files.
- **Persistent Artifacts**: Revisions, syntheses, and entity links are preserved and grow richer as more knowledge is ingested.
- **CS Study Integration**: Acts as a repository for your CS50, C++, and DSA study guides, mapping, and reference implementations.
