# AI Skills Yatra — Summer Sprint Timeline (6-Day Plan)

Since you are on summer break and can dedicate **3-4 hours per day**, you can complete the entire 18-hour program (1,073 minutes total) in just **6 days**! This accelerated schedule groups the courses logically to keep daily study times balanced.
